Understanding Suicide

Suicide is a major public health concern both in Ireland and worldwide. It affects individuals, families, and communities.

Key Facts

  • Suicide is one of the leading causes of death among young adults
  • Men are statistically more likely to die by suicide, while women report higher rates of attempts
  • Mental health issues such as depression and anxiety are significant risk factors

Suicide Prevention

Many suicides are preventable. Early intervention, education, and access to mental health services can save lives.

  1. Encouraging open conversations
  2. Reducing stigma around mental health
  3. Improving access to support services

Important Note

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, contact emergency services or reach out to a support organisation listed on the Resources page.
