Welcome

Mental health is just as important as physical health. This website aims to raise awareness, reduce stigma, and provide helpful support and resources for those who may be struggling.

What Is Mental Health?

Mental health affects how we think, feel, and act. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.
